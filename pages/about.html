About

Katherine Donnally is a ceramic artist based in Richmond, Virginia who focuses on functional, artistic stoneware pottery. She has a background in Visual & Popular American Studies as well as graphic design, and incorporates these inspirations into her pieces. Katherine's work is defined by an eye-catching graphic quality, bringing classic forms to life with hand-painted designs, underglaze transfers, and crisp black-and-white patterns. Her goal as an artist is to make artwork that people look forward to using in their everyday lives.
